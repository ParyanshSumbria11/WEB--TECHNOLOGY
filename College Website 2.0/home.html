Welcome to GCET Jammu

Welcome to Government College of Engineering and Technology, Jammu. GCET Jammu is dedicated to providing quality technical education and developing skilled and responsible engineers.

Campus & Academic Highlights

Established in 1993 and spread across 869 kanals at Chak Bhalwal, Jammu, GCET Jammu is an Autonomous College approved by AICTE and affiliated with the University of Jammu, offering B.Tech programs across Computer Science, Electronics & Communication, Electrical, Mechanical, and Civil Engineering.
