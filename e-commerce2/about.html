About ShopEase

A simple approach to online shopping.

OUR STORY

Shopping Made Easier for Everyone

ShopEase is a student-created online shopping website designed to make browsing products simple and enjoyable. It brings different product categories together in one place.

From electronics and clothing to accessories and footwear, users can explore products, check their prices, and add their favourite items to a shopping cart.

Our main goal is to create a clean, organised, and user-friendly website using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
